Conjuring

A film treatment about a fallen mental magician who is asked to uncover a crime inside a secluded estate.

A treatment of Film story:

Conjuring

As a mental magician, I have had pretty bad luck. I don't even have money to buy food and mainly rely on attending various friends' gatherings to solve my eating problems. At these gatherings, I perform mental magic tricks, such as accurately revealing the other person's secret first love, and receive some cheap applause, then quietly enjoying my meal and leaving.

Ten years ago, I was a famous mental magician. I was once very popular in Japan and appeared on variety shows with Takeshi Kitano. After returning to China, I also performed with Dr. Henry Chang-Yu Lee (a famous American detective). However, I discovered that mental magicians face prejudice from the public due to their craft being perceived as a mere parlor trick, unworthy of high society. Soon my halo faded away and slowly forgotten by the public. What really hit me was my personal life because I found out that my girlfriend of many years had an affair. Although I had previously negotiated with my romantic rival, he attacked me with a knife and damaged the nerve in my right leg. As a result, I still require crutches to walk.

Yesterday, I received an anonymous letter inviting me to a seaside mansion to solve the owner's private problem. The letter did not include a signature or name, but the author had prepaid five thousand dollars into my bank account beforehand. The author promised that if I could solve their problem, they would use their top-level network connections to help me return into the entertainment industry and make me successful again.

The letter seemed like a prank, but it mentioned my book on "mind reading" from over ten years ago, which made it convincing. The author admired the book and used it to find me. I value this point because I worked hard on the book despite its poor sales. Therefore, meeting with someone who appreciates my efforts and can bring success is reasonable. In any case, I no longer want to live like what I did.

It took me ten hours on the road and when I arrived at the mansion, I found it was a semi-closed area of ​​a cold coast. There is only one huge mansion deep into the peninsula. Several carefully trimmed Bauhaus-style large buildings and a huge courtyard covering hundreds of acres. On the right side of the mansion is a huge beach with exposed rocks around it, full of various caves and small sandbars.

The weather was very bad that day, with strong winds mixed with heavy rain. Four hundred dollars out of five thousand dollars that I received were spent on taxi fare because according to the taxi driver there are hardly any people coming here.

The owner of the letter is a cold and indifferent middle-aged man named Li. His voice is low, and his demeanor is troubled, but I believe he has enough power to fulfill his promise. He told me that over a week ago, his five-year-old daughter Nina was sexually assaulted by someone. The traumatized Nina can no longer speak and maintains a terrible silence in response to any inquiries from anyone. His private doctor confirmed the fact of sexual assault but found no fingerprints or semen or other evidence that could identify the perpetrator. Although her physical condition may not be serious, Nina's soul has suffered severe trauma.

He did not choose to report it to the police because this matter is absolutely private for him. He owns several huge listed companies, and any problems with his family will cause huge fluctuations in stock prices. But the main reason is that he wants to protect his daughter's privacy because he does not believe that the police can keep it confidential. If any law enforcement officers appear near his home, journalists who have always been interested in him will spread rumors.

He said he had read my book and one of his good friends strongly recommended me to him without revealing their name. But I believe it must be someone who saw my performance on site. He hopes that through my mind-reading skills, I can decipher Nina's inner secrets while she cannot speak normally due to her trauma and find the culprit.

Finally, he told me that in another week they would immigrate with their family to UK and never come back again. He hoped Ie could find out who did it within a week. If successful, I would receive $50k from him, and he also contact TV stations for making an exclusive show just for me. I later learned that there are several huge media platforms under his name which makes this possible.

We have made some agreements:

1)I must keep confidentiality whether I successfully find out who did it.

2)I can freely enter and exit the estate and nearby areas but cannot leave an area within 30 kilometers from here.

3)I cannot cause any physical or mental harm to Nina.

4)I can ask anyone in this estate, including his Japanese wife, a petite and pitiful mother named Hanako Tabe.

5)After knowing who it did, I must secretly tell Mr. Li without telling anyone else.

Mr. Ye, the butler, gave me a list of all the people who have been in and out of this estate from last week until now. Most of them are inside the estate, while some live in a small town not far from it.

The list is as follows:

1. Mr. Li, 55 years old, owner of the estate and a wealthy entrepreneur.

2. Mrs. Tabe, 33 years old, Mr. Li's second wife.

3. Mr. Ye, 50 years old, butler.

4. Ms. Li Minghao ,40 years old, Mr. Li’s younger sister who has been living with her brother on the estate unmarried.

5.Mr. Tsuyoshi, 28 years old, Mrs.Tabe's younger brother who came to visit his sister at the estate last month

6.Nina, 5-year-old daughter of Mr. Li and Mrs. Tabe

7.Li Yuan ,17-year-old daughter of Mr. Li and his ex-wife

8.Ye Hui ,19-year-old son of the butler

9.Mr. Ron Blunt ,39-year-old private doctor for Mr. Li; divorced; also serves as Li Yuan's tutor.

10.Mrs Liu, wife of Butler Ye, mother to Ye Hui

11.Tom, the seven-year-old son Ron Blunt, and Nina’s good friend

Outside this list are workers here and household servants such as maidservants or footmen. They all reside in subsidiary buildings outside gradually or near manor. There are also some delivery personnel who come here to deliver flowers and food supplies.

On my first day there I spent time familiarizing myself with my surroundings and talking to everyone on that list. I learned about their backgrounds and relationships. I soon discovered that there were various complex relationships within this huge estate. Firstly, everyone admits that a few days before the sexual assault, Mr. Li had a quarrel with his wife's younger brother, Mr. Tsuyoshi. No one knows what happened, but they could hear Mrs. Tabe crying. Mr. Li simply said he hated people asking him for money.

I soon found out that whenever I asked Mr. Li a question, he would always ask me, “Don’t you know how to read minds? You don't need me to say more; you already know what I'm thinking, right?" This left me almost speechless. Is there really such a thing as mind reading? This is a very complicated topic. Afterwards, Mr. Li didn’t talk to me anymore and seemed addicted to fishing all day long.

I met Nina. She was holding a spotted stuffed toy and wearing clothes that she hadn't changed for over a week. She refused to talk to anyone or let anyone get close to her body. I spent a lot of time following her. She used to like playing on the beach and hiding in the rock caves by the bay, but now she's curled up motionless in the greenhouse garden of the estate.

I was led by her playmate, an interesting little boy named Tom, and discovered her small secret kingdom near a cave on the beach. It was a children's playground decorated with candles, graffiti, and various toys. The builders were Nina and her friend Tom, who used to spend time playing inside it. And this is where sexual assault occurred. There is nothing suspicious here; the rising tide flooded the ground and washed away all footprints.

Using mental magic techniques, I was able to ease Nina's initial hostility towards me. Although she remained silent, she began listening to what I had to say. Mental magic theory suggests creating a world of childlike innocence and security that closely resembles the day of the sexual assault, but with all evil or terrifying elements replaced by safe things.

To achieve this, I created a fairy tale world where Nina was the happy protagonist surrounded by cute characters who represented potential suspects in the estate. The incident of sexual assault was replaced with an insignificant chase game in this story. My goal is to observe how Nina reacts to this game and identify the culprit.

Although Nina may provide an answer, it is still unclear whether it is truthful. Therefore, I will need to use my mental magic technique on each suspect to determine if they are lying. The process is straightforward: whoever lies must be the culprit.

During my discussions with various individuals, I gradually unraveled the intricate web of relationships that existed between them. For instance, Ron, who had served as a family tutor for Li Yuan, appeared to have an enigmatic bond with the young girl. Some even speculated that they had received Mr. Li's tacit approval for their relationship. Additionally, Mr. Li’s sister, Li Minghao seemed to harbor deep affection towards Ron which further complicated matters between the three of them.

As for Mrs.Tabe's younger brother - he is an indolent man who has been living off his sister and has a reputation for being promiscuous towards all women including his own flesh and blood!

In this grand mansion, the ghostly presence of Jasmine, Mr. Li's former spouse, still lingers. It appears that aside from Mr. Li and his devoted butler, everyone holds deep affection and remorse for her. Interestingly enough, I uncovered that it was actually Jasmine who built Mr. Li's thriving business empire before she tragically perished in a shipwreck at sea fourteen years ago when their eldest daughter, Li Yuan, was only five years old.

Amidst whispers of the ghostly presence of Mrs. Jasmine, many servants and workers believe that Nina's misfortune at the tender age of five was no mere coincidence. The unspoken insinuation hangs heavy in the air, casting a shadow over all who dare to speak her name. In this atmosphere of uncertainty and fear, Mrs. Tabe's vulnerability only served to draw me closer to her pitiful plight.

Instead of believing in rumors, I concluded that Ye Hui, the butler's son, was responsible for sexual assault.

In truth, Ye Hui is emotionally estranged and happens to be an illegitimate offspring conceived from a liaison between Jasmine, Mr. Li's ex-wife, and Butler Ye. The butler has always concealed this confidential matter and extorted Mr. Li in private; otherwise, he would expose this disgraceful revelation to the public eye.

Ye Hui gradually developed a solitary and eccentric personality due to being neglected by both his father (Butler Ye) who knew about his true identity as well as Liu (the wife of Butler Ye), who abused him since childhood because she despised him as merely "a nameless son".

Ye Hui had only heard rumors and had a vague intuition about his true identity. He later discovered that Mrs. Jasmine's substantial wealth was managed by a trust fund, which he unknowingly signed away several weeks prior under false pretenses, relinquishing any inheritance rights. The property was secretly divided between Mr. Li and Butler Ye. Upon learning this truth, Ye Hui sought revenge through sexual assault.

I penned a letter to the esteemed Mr. Li, informing him of my discovery of the culprit and my decision to depart without bidding adieu. It is abundantly clear that upon learning of this familial scandal, Mr. Li and I shall never be able to meet eye-to-eye again. Nevertheless, it is imperative that my benefactor remains unaware of this development. The fate of my future success or downfall lies solely in the hands of Mr. Li's influential power.

On the second day after my departure, I received news of a tragic shipwreck. Mr. Li, Ye Hui, and his father - Ye the butler had perished while fishing near the estate. It was evident to me that Mr. Li sought revenge against Ye Hui and his butler for extorting him over the years; however, an unfortunate accident occurred resulting in their deaths.

Following this incident, I chose to lead a reclusive life, as many mysteries surrounded Mr. Li's fishing boat on which they met their fate. My suspicions even extended towards Mr. Li’s ex-wife who may have also died on that boat.

A week later, I received a $45,000 check which rightfully belonged to me since I used my intellect to solve the case without being responsible for "morality."

Simultaneously, I received an invitation from a television channel inviting me to appear on a variety show. Mr. Li seems to be a man of his word no matter how you look at it. I feel like I recognize the voice on the phone. However, despite its tempting success prospects, something felt unsettling about my entire reasoning process feeling too "logical."

A few days later, I was curled up in my small office and dozed off when a guest arrived. When I opened my eyes, I saw the pitiful Mrs. Tabe and another person, Ron, who came in hand in hand with her. The first thing Ron said when he entered the room was that he was the one who recommended me to Mr. Li.

As I gazed at his face, a sudden realization hit me. The man standing before me was not Ron Blunt but Toby, the renowned magician who had retired from the industry years ago after being exposed on stage. He had fallen into obscurity for over a decade since then.

Ron told me that he and his lover Mrs. Tabe inherited Mr. Li's company and plan to return to England with their newfound wealth to build their own entertainment empire. He also mentioned reclaiming the place in the world he had lost before.

So, I had an epiphany and realized the truth. The estate was like a chamber of illusions crafted by a masterful mental magician, deceiving my every perception. Ron, however, found it effortless to navigate this labyrinth of deception.

Contrary to what was initially suspected, there was no instance of sexual assault whatsoever. As a family physician, Ron conducted a thorough examination and advised against involving law enforcement. In fact, this scheme had been meticulously planned by both Ron and Mrs. Tabe for years on end.

Their ultimate goal was to inherit all of Mr. Li and his ex-wife Jasmine's assets without leaving any evidence behind. To achieve this, they planned to use me as a tool to induce Mr. Li, butler Ye, and Ye Hui to die together. They took advantage of butler Ye's greediness by secretly transferring Jasmine's assets to Mr. Li before revealing his revenge plan through butler Ye in order to gain his help later on.

Additionally, they tampered with the ship early on from fishing activities at sea. Finally, they provided evidence to the police that butler Ye and his wife were planning to kill Mr. Li so that all responsibility would fall onto them who died at sea along with their son.

All of this has no evidence left behind as everyone who knew what happened perished at sea.

Ron neither denied nor confirmed my statement. He simply said that he was there to fulfill Mr. Li's promise and create a TV program for me. He wanted to tell the world that the mental reading performance was real, not a scam.

But I refused. I admitted my failure. I had no face left to perform.

I only had one request: I wanted to meet Nina. I didn't believe that a five-year-old girl could fool me with her acting skills. I didn't think she was performing from start to finish; I needed to see it with my own eyes.

Ron told me that every word Nina spoke was taught by him, and for her, it was just like playing a game with her good friend Tom. The best actors in the world are children.

I closed my office and continued attending those endless dinner parties and eating, making everyone happy and leaving alone like before.
