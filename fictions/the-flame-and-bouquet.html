The Flame and Bouquet

Chapter 1 opens in a freezing inn, with rain, mead, and the sense that a long journey is just beginning.

原文链接 Open the original Medium post nordenbox.medium.com/the-flame-and-bouquet-5fa2439584b1

On the night the narrator arrives at Rosewood Inn, the building appears through the mist like a temporary refuge from the storm. The whole place is cold, damp, and already half-surrendered to the weather.

The room is crowded with monks, merchants, and the smell of wet wool. A cup of house mead becomes the first small gesture of hospitality.

Outside, the mountain wind keeps pressing against the windows. The inn becomes a threshold space where travel, memory, and uncertainty all gather at once.
