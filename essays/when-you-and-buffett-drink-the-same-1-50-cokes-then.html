When You and Buffett Drink the Same $1.50 Cokes, Then…

A Coke can look equal in price while ideological status becomes the new luxury good.

原文链接 Open the original Medium post nordenbox.medium.com/when-you-and-buffett-drink-1-50-cokes-then-e4b7678b9ade

When the industrial world makes a $1.50 Coke look equally available to everyone, the old visual markers of wealth start to collapse. A billionaire and an ordinary person can hold the same tin can.

That creates a new anxiety for elites: if money no longer buys obvious distinction, what can prove superiority?

The article argues that the answer becomes belief. Luxury is no longer only about consumption; it becomes a set of expensive moral positions, class-coded values, and public rituals of self-justification.

From there the essay widens into a critique of modern ideological enforcement in entertainment, finance, and culture, where “correctness” itself becomes a form of status and gatekeeping.
