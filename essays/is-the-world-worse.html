Is the world worse?

A rainy conversation by a dying campfire turns into a reflection on how communication technologies shrink the world while making it louder.

原文链接 Open the original Medium post nordenbox.medium.com/is-the-world-worse-5a5c5a15c3ee

The essay begins with a simple question asked on a rainy day in Victoria, BC: has the world become worse?

Behind that question is the sense that communication technology has made everyone more connected, but also more chaotic, and that people now talk confidently about matters far beyond their actual experience.

From that starting point, the piece weighs contemporary disorientation against older, smaller social worlds, then asks whether the feeling of decline is really a decline in the world or a change in perception.
