Chinese Middle-Aged Men and Alcohol: Navigating a Complex Relationship

A social scene built around food, cigarettes, and baijiu, and the emotional logic behind it.

原文链接 Open the original Medium post nordenbox.medium.com/chinese-middle-aged-men-and-alcohol-navigating-a-complex-relationship-b67bcc8072cf

The article opens with a familiar modern Chinese table: fatty food, spicy dishes, cigarettes, and strong liquor, usually surrounded by political talk and loud social confidence.

That scene is not accidental. It condenses a specific social code, in which middle-aged men and baijiu remain tied together as a kind of default cultural pairing.

The writer then turns inward, tracing how resentment toward drinking slowly changed over time until the relationship looked less like disgust and more like a complicated social reality.
