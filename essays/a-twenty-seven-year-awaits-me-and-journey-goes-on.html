A Twenty-Seven-Year Awaits Me and Journey Goes On

A long-distance love letter to Final Fantasy VII Rebirth, and to the 27 years that led back to it.

原文链接 Open the original Medium post medium.com/a-twenty-seven-year-awaits-me-and-journey-goes-on-9733e1fe02e9

The final scene of Final Fantasy VII Rebirth.

Heart-wrenching, grief-stricken, and bewildered-that was the feeling at the end, when the last cinematic cutscene played on a 65-inch TV in a Montreal basement at 2:30 AM, with snow falling silently outside.

Twenty-seven years earlier, the writer first stumbled into Final Fantasy VII in a film school dormitory, where a CRT screen was flashing ugly, comical polygons and everyone around it was screaming with excitement. The obsession started there.

The point is not just nostalgia. The piece keeps returning to why this series matters: the world-building, the steam-punk atmosphere, the recurring Ghibli-like visual mood, and the emotional grammar of friendship, loss, sacrifice, and sincerity.
