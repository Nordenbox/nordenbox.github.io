Why I believed that <Suzume> was not superior to <Your Name>

A comparison of narrative drivers, gendered protagonists, and what modern audiences now ask a story to do.

原文链接 Open the original Medium post nordenbox.medium.com/why-i-believed-that-suzume-was-not-superior-to-your-name-4bcaf1dced0

The article begins by asking why Japanese animation so often places women at the center of the story, and why that shift feels natural to contemporary viewers.

Its answer is structural: older narrative machines ran on goals, obstacles, and conquest, while newer ones move through self-questioning, explanation, and renewed uncertainty.

From there the comparison with Your Name and Suzume becomes a way of thinking about what modern stories now value: not just plot movement, but inward reconfiguration.
